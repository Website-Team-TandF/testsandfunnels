Questions about our process

What happens after I request the audit?

We contact you to collect your brief first. We then audit your industry and your full funnel, pitch a strategy and action plan to fix the gaps, and share the prerequisites and the roadmap for onboarding and execution.

Do I need to be running ads already?

No. The audit covers visibility, lead capture, nurturing, management and retention, and the form has an option for businesses that are not running ads yet.

Where do the AI agents come in?

At step 3. The strategy names which manual steps our agents take over first, so automation starts where it saves the most effort.
