When to use AI, and when to shoot

The production plan says which is which, asset by asset.

AI visuals suit

  • Concept testing before a shoot is booked.
  • Variations of an ad that already works.
  • Scenes that are costly or slow to shoot.

A shoot suits

  • Products where the exact look must be accurate.
  • Brand footage you will reuse for a long time.
  • Anything that must show a real place, person or result.

How we keep it safe

  • A person reviews every AI visual before it goes into an ad.
  • Product, brand and colour accuracy are checked against the real product.
  • Platform rules on AI-generated content in ads are checked before launch.
  • For hospitals and clinics, we do not use AI visuals to show patients or treatment results.

Questions about AI visuals and video

Is AI-generated content allowed in ads?

Platforms set rules on AI-generated content in ads, including labelling in some cases. We check the current rules for each platform before anything goes live.

Can AI visuals replace a shoot?

Sometimes, for concepts and variations. Where the exact look of the product matters, we recommend a shoot, and the production plan says which assets are which.
