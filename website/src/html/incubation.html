Incube8: an incubator for training, validation, acceleration, office space and funding

Incube8 is a startup incubator in Delhi NCR for ventures that need to test an idea, and ventures with traction that are not yet at the scale to invest in marketing, advertising or sales on their own. t&f is one of three partners behind it.

Incube8 is an incubator and accelerator for ventures from any industry, with a strong suit in medical and hospitals. It offers training, validation, acceleration, office space and funding, and is backed by VivoCorp, GeMTech PARAS and t&f.

What incubated ventures get

Training

Performance marketing, brand and investor-pitch skills for your team, from our training program.

Validation

A structured test of demand, positioning and unit economics with real customer data, using the same tests-and-funnels method we run for clients.

Acceleration

Marketing, advertising, sales and production support from t&f, with ongoing strategy and accountability, so a validated venture can scale.

Office space

A desk and production-studio access in our Delhi NCR office.

Funding

Seed and growth capital, structured to your venture's stage.

Who it is for

Two ways in

  • Validate: you have an idea, a prototype or a pilot and want to test demand before you spend.
  • Accelerate: you have traction but not yet the scale to invest in marketing, advertising or sales on your own.
  • Any industry. Our strong suit is medical and hospitals.

Questions about Incube8

Who can apply to Incube8?

Ventures from any industry, from an idea or prototype that needs validation to a venture with real traction that needs to accelerate. Medical and hospital ventures are our strong suit.

What does t&f contribute?

Marketing and production, driven by professional marketers and AI agents, alongside the capital from VivoCorp and the tendering support from GeMTech PARAS.

What does validation mean in Incube8?

Testing whether customers want what you are building, before you scale it: demand, positioning and unit economics, measured with real customer data.

Is every application taken forward?

No. We review each application before anything is agreed.
