Questions about influencer marketing

How are creators chosen?

By fit with your audience and your goal, and by the quality of their content. A person reviews the shortlist before anyone is contacted.

How do you measure influencer campaigns?

Each creator gets their own link or code, so clicks, leads and sales can be traced to the creator and the post.

Can influencer content be used in my ads?

Yes, with the creator's permission, and we agree usage rights in the brief.
