Production plan

Lines up the shoots and assets the calendar needs, so production is planned before it is booked.

ORM

Questions about social media agents

Can the publisher post without my approval?

It publishes what has been approved. The calendar and the content are reviewed by your point of contact at t&f before they go out, and you set the limits the agents work within.

Do agents reply to comments on their own?

The ORM agent handles comments and responses within templates you approve. Conversations that need a person are taken by a person.
