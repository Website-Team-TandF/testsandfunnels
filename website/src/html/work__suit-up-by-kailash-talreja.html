What the content looks like

  • One teacher, one workbench. Master Kailash Talreja at the cutting table, explaining one step of cutting or drafting per Reel: the sleeve, the armhole, the pocket, the hem, the front draft, the trouser.
  • Real measurements. Captions give the actual numbers, such as a 36-inch chest and a 23.5-inch sleeve, so each Reel can be followed on cloth.
  • Short and varied. The latest 12 Reels run from 19 to 103 seconds.
  • The two Reels above 400K plays are a cutting rule many tailors skip, and a then-versus-now comparison of the shirt front.
  • The family's craft sayings are part of the series, passed down from Master Kanhaiya Lal Talreja.

What we did

Personal branding and influencer creation

Built the account around one master teaching the craft, in a series he can repeat, and ran the content system behind it.

Personal branding and influencer creation

Brand identity and collateral

Logo, letterhead, price card, packaging sticker and an interlining label that carries the house's story, so the brand looks the same on the cloth, in the shop and on screen.

Founder-story video

A video on how Master Kailash Talreja learned the craft, to give the series its backstory.

Operations dashboard

An order dashboard that links each customer to their orders and each garment to the team member making it, and calculates what is paid and pending.
