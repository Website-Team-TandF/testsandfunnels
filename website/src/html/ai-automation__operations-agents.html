What the operations agents do

MIS and lead dashboard

Kept current together with your sales team.

  • Leads by source
  • Follow-up status
  • From first touch to close

Reporting

Reports that follow the funnel, stage by stage.

  • Impressions, clicks, leads, sales and revenue
  • What each rupee of spend produced

Production and order planning

Plans that turn the calendar or the order book into work.

  • Shoots and assets lined up before they are booked
  • Orders assigned to the people who will make them

Order and customer tracking

One record from customer to order to item.

  • Customer, order and item linked
  • Payments received and pending calculated
  • Each item assigned to one team member

Operations in practice

For Suit Up by Kailash Talreja, a Delhi bespoke tailor, the order dashboard links each customer to their orders and each garment to the team member making it, and calculates what has been paid and what is pending.

Questions about operations agents

What counts as operations automation?

The repeating back-office work around marketing and sales: MIS, lead and order dashboards, reporting and planning. The audit marks which of these steps are still done by hand and could move to an agent.

What is in the MIS and lead dashboard?

It tracks leads from first touch to close: where each came from, what follow-up has happened and what the outcome was, kept current with your sales team.
