Questions about personal branding

Who is personal branding for?

Founders, professionals, experts and masters of a craft who win clients on their name, and ventures built around one person.

What is influencer creation?

Building a person into a creator: a clear position, a content format they can repeat, direction on camera and a production and publishing system, so their audience grows around what they know.

Which channels do you focus on?

Instagram, LinkedIn, YouTube and Google, including AI search, because that is where most personal brands are found and judged.

Will the content sound like me?

Scripts and posts are drafted in your voice, and you approve them before they go out.

How do you measure a personal brand?

By qualified enquiries and audience growth, not likes alone, with each enquiry tracked from first touch.
