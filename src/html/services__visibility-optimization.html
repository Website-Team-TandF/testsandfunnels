Questions about visibility optimization

What are AEO and GEO?

AEO structures content so answer engines and voice assistants can give a direct answer. GEO makes your brand and pages easier for generative AI systems to understand and cite.

Do you guarantee rankings or AI citations?

No. Search engines and AI systems do not offer guarantees, so we do not promise rankings, citations, traffic or revenue. We show the work and the measurement.
