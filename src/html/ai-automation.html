Questions about AI automation

How is this different from a regular digital marketing agency?

A regular agency staffs each task with a person. We run the repetitive tasks with agents and keep people on strategy and review, so you manage one contact and less of your budget goes into execution.

Will AI-written content sound generic?

Agents draft, and people review what goes live against your brand voice and the message templates you approve. Your point of contact owns the strategy, not the tool.

Do I have to automate everything at once?

No. The audit marks which manual steps to move to agents first, and we begin there.

Can AI agents automate sales and operations, not just marketing?

Yes. Sales agents handle lead capture, follow-up and the workflows around tele-calls. Operations agents keep MIS, lead dashboards, reports and plans current. Each starts from the steps the audit marks as manual.
