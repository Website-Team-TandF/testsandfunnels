The tests and funnels method

tests

Smart experimentation. You run A/B tests through data analysis to find what works in ads, landing pages and messaging, so performance keeps improving and ROI keeps growing.

funnels

Every business needs a funnel built around its own ideal customer, value proposition and challenges. You learn to turn impressions into clicks, and clicks into loyal customers, with minimal friction.

Questions about the program

Is the program online or in-office?

Both. You can attend online live or in person at the VivoCorp Workspace. Both formats follow the same curriculum, and you choose one when you apply.

Where are the in-office sessions held?

At the VivoCorp Workspace, a co-working and incubation space run by VivoCorp India Private Limited.

How is AI taught in the program?

AI is part of every module: AI in ads, AI in SEO, AI in social media, AI in email and WhatsApp marketing, and AI in digital sales. You practise with tools such as ChatGPT, Claude, NotebookLM and Google AI Studio on real campaign work.

Do I get a certificate?

Yes. Certificates are part of the program.

Is a job guaranteed?

No. Placement and client-acquisition support covers portfolio building and case studies, interview preparation, client brief collection, 360° marketing strategy and client pitch presentation. It is support, not a job guarantee.

Which tools will I learn?

Website, analytics, advertising, SEO, CRM and AI tools, including Google Ads, Meta Ads, Google Tag Manager, Shopify, Zoho CRM, ChatGPT and Claude.

How do I pay, and is EMI available?

The fee is ₹40,000 + GST for online live and ₹60,000 + GST for in-office. EMI is available on major credit cards and on HDFC debit cards, with tenures that depend on your bank.

What is the refund policy?

Read our Refund Policy.

How long is the program, and when does the next batch start?

The program is 41 sessions of 1 to 2 hours each, about five a week, so it runs inside 2 months. The next intake starts on Monday 7 December 2026 and applications close on Friday 27 November 2026. A new intake starts about every 2 months.

How many seats are there?

In-office intakes are limited to 6 students, each chosen after a one-on-one interaction. Online has no cap.

Is there homework?

Yes. Every session ends with a task, usually 1 to 2 hours, that you can do at home or in the office. The next session starts with a review of it.
