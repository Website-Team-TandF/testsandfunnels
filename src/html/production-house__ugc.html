Questions about UGC

What is UGC?

UGC means user-generated content: content made by customers or creators in a natural, first-person style, rather than a studio ad. Brands use it in ads and on social because audiences tend to trust it more than polished brand creative.

Can UGC run as a paid ad?

Yes, with the creator's permission, and usage rights are agreed in the brief. On Meta, partnership ads let a brand run an ad through a creator's handle.

How many UGC videos do I need?

Enough to test several hooks and angles against each other. The plan sets the number from your goal and your budget, and the results decide which ones to make more of.
