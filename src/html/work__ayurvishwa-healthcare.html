How the 815 enquiries were sorted

A lead total alone says little about how many people can be booked, so the report shows the status of every enquiry. A little over one in four was marked qualified, and more than four in ten had no status recorded.

Questions about this case study

What did tests&funnels do for Ayurvishwa Healthcare?

We ran Meta lead campaigns for its clinics and visiting-doctor days, tracked enquiries through to patient visits, planned Google Ads keywords and ad copy, audited its website for SEO and made ad and social creatives.

What is a visiting OPD campaign?

In the campaign reports, a visiting OPD is an outpatient day a doctor holds at a branch on a set date, and a standalone OPD is a fixed clinic. A visiting-OPD campaign advertises the day in that town so people can enquire before it.

Does this case study claim any treatment results?

No. It reports enquiries, qualified enquiries, patient visits and cost per lead. It makes no claim about health outcomes, and it shows no patient data.

Why were only 221 of 815 enquiries marked qualified?

357 enquiries had no status recorded and 168 were marked not specific. We show the full split because a lead total on its own overstates how many people can be booked.
