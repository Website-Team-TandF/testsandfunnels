Questions from hospitals and clinics

Can hospitals and clinics advertise on Meta and Google?

Yes, within each platform's healthcare advertising policies. Some treatments and claims are restricted, and we plan creative around the restrictions.

Yes. Enquiries are followed up on email and WhatsApp, and tracked so you can see how many become appointments.

Which metric matters most for a clinic?

Cost per qualified enquiry and appointments booked, because they tie spend to patients who actually arrive.
