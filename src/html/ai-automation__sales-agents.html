Questions about sales agents

Do the agents call my leads?

The tele-call agents run the workflows around calling your leads. People take the conversations that need a person.

Can agents replace my sales team?

No. Agents handle the repetitive follow-up and the workflows around calls. People take the conversations that need a person, and our sales services cover hands-on lead conversion and training.
