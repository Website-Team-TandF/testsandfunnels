Questions about our production house

Is production done in-house?

Photography, videography, content, design and websites are handled in-house, and agents handle scripting, content calendars, production plans and video editing.

What does realistic media creation mean?

Photo, video and graphics that show your product, place or person as they really are, made either with a shoot or with AI visuals that match the real thing. A person reviews every asset before it goes live.

Can one shoot serve Meta, Google and LinkedIn ads?

That is how we plan it. The storyboard lists where each asset will run and in which format, so one shoot feeds several channels.

Do you produce media for hospitals and clinics?

Yes, within the advertising rules that apply to healthcare. We do not use patient data or make treatment-outcome claims.
