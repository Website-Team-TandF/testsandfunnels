Questions before you ask for an audit

What does t&f do?

t&f is three things under one roof: an AI agency that automates marketing, sales and operations with AI agents; a production house for realistic media, UGC, influencer marketing and personal branding; and a partner in Incube8, an incubator that offers training, validation, acceleration, office space and funding.

What does t&f automate with AI?

Marketing: scripting, content calendars, production plans, video editing, publishing, insights review, influencer outreach, comment and response management, and ad setup, optimization and reporting. Sales: lead generation and management, follow-up and tele-calling workflows. Operations: MIS and lead dashboards, reporting and planning. People at t&f set the strategy and review what goes live. We build on Claude, Higgsfield and ChatGPT.

Do you only automate, or do you make the media too?

Both. Photography, videography, graphics, UGC and AI visuals are produced for your ads and channels, and agents handle scripting, planning and editing around them, so more of your budget goes into the media itself and less into managing its production.

Who will I talk to?

One person. You talk to a single point of contact at t&f, who coordinates the agents and our team, so you are not managing separate people for content, ads, leads and reporting.

Who stays in control of my ad spend and customer messages?

You and our team do. Agents work within limits you approve, such as budgets, audiences and message templates. People at t&f review what goes live and take the conversations that need a person.

Is t&f also an incubator?

t&f is a partner in Incube8, an incubator backed by VivoCorp and GeMTech PARAS. It offers training, validation, acceleration, office space and funding to ventures. See the Incube8 page for who it is for and how to apply.

Which industries do you work in?

E-commerce brands, hospitals and clinics, and personal brands. Personal branding, influencer marketing and UGC are also available on their own.
